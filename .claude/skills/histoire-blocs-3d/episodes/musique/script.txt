In my town, there's a monster that can only move when the music stops. If it goes quiet for even one second... it's over for all of us.

Three years ago, this thing appeared in the middle of our town square. That night, nine people disappeared.

The next morning, a kid walked past it, blasting his radio. And the monster froze. That's how we found its weakness.

So the town built six giant speaker towers around it. Each one has its own backup generator. And every night, twelve guards stand watch with portable speakers.

For three years, the music never stopped. And the monster never moved. Until last night.

My dad is the head of security, and he finally let me join his night shift.

Everything was normal. Until three oh seven A.M.

[excited] One of the towers exploded!

"Relax," my dad said. "We still have five more."

Then his radio crackled. The backup generators were failing. One by one, the towers went silent.

[shouting] My dad screamed, "Everyone, make noise! Now!"

Police cars surrounded the square, sirens blasting. People opened their windows and played music from their phones.

I thought we were safe. Until thunder crashed, and heavy rain started pouring down.

The windows closed. The sirens died. And the guards' speakers started shorting out in the rain.

Soon, mine was the last speaker still playing. And then... my battery died.

[whispering] For one second, the square went completely silent.

A horrible creaking sound echoed through the rain.

My dad turned his speaker back on. But the monster wasn't in the middle of the square anymore.

It was standing right behind him.

It raised one enormous hand. And my dad whispered, [whispering] "Whatever happens... don't let the music stop."
