The monster froze... with its hand one inch above my dad's head.

Nobody moved. The only sound in the whole square was my dad's tiny speaker. And the battery was at two percent.

My dad slowly crouched down... and crawled out from under the hand.

Then he pointed across the square. At the ice cream truck.

Every ice cream truck in our town has a giant speaker on the roof. And the song plays on repeat.

I didn't think. I just ran.

I jumped over the barrier. I slid under a police car.

Then lightning hit tower number four.

[shouting] It started falling. Right towards me!

I dove to the side, and it smashed into the ground so hard the whole square shook.

Sparks. Glass. Smoke everywhere.

But the crash was so loud, the monster stayed frozen.

I reached the truck. The door was locked.

Behind me, my dad was yelling. His speaker was dying.

I smashed the window with my elbow, climbed in, and turned the key.

Nothing.

I turned it again.

The engine roared, and the speaker on the roof started blasting the ice cream song.

One second later, my dad's speaker died.

The monster didn't move. We did it.

I drove the truck right up next to it, as close as I dared.

My dad jumped in, soaking wet, and for the first time all night... he laughed.

Then the song ended.

I looked at the screen on the dashboard.

It said... Repeat. Off.

[whispering] And the monster's head... slowly turned towards us.
